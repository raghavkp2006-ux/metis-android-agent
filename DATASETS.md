# Datasets

No language or behavioral dataset has been collected or shipped in Phase 1. There are no real names, messages, audio recordings, notification contents, contact exports, or user timelines in test fixtures.

The [dataset contract](docs/contracts/DATASET.md) defines CSV fields, UTF-16 entity spans, family-based 70/15/15 splits, required negative/ambiguous/out-of-scope cases, provenance, privacy, and evaluation. The [intent catalog](docs/PRODUCT_CONTRACT.md) contains 32 planned intent labels; enabled support is incremental and requires evaluation, not merely a catalog entry.

Phase 7 includes a versioned [45-case synthetic development regression corpus](docs/evaluation/english-rules-v1.txt) for a bounded English grammar. It was authored with the rules and has no training/validation/test split; it is not an independent held-out accuracy evaluation. Its expected intents/outcomes use a fixed clock, zone and synthetic read port. [Phase 7 verification](docs/PHASE_7_VERIFICATION.md) records scope and limits. ML improvements are deferred to Phase 15 and must beat the same baseline on independent utterance families. Dataset contributions require explicit opt-in and redaction; no private content is collected automatically.

Phase 15 adds a corrected [synthetic intent corpus](docs/evaluation/phase15/intent-v2.csv), a [50-case safety/resolution corpus](docs/evaluation/phase15/safety-v2.csv), and a reproducible native-baseline evaluator. Intent rows split 1,130/240/230 across 110/24/23 disjoint family groups, approximately 70/15/15 after merging similar template stems and rounding per label. The 240 validation rows split again by family into 80 calibration and 160 threshold-selection examples. This is single-author synthetic development data with prior exploratory exposure, not an independent blind benchmark. The [Phase 15 report](docs/PHASE_15_VERIFICATION.md) records a safety-driven no-go; the candidate was not packaged or enabled.

The [v3 read-query corpus](docs/evaluation/phase15/read_queries_v3/intent-v3.csv) has 7,200 fictional development examples: 5,110 train, 370 calibration, 720 threshold/model-selection, and 1,000 test. It reuses exposed v2 positive templates and keeps each base prompt and its adversarial wrappers in one partition. Four read intents plus UNKNOWN are evaluated; creation remains outside learned scope. This is development data, not independent evidence. The [125-row review package](docs/evaluation/phase15/read_queries_v3/review-package.csv) contains 100 unapproved drafts and 25 user-contributed challenges; no full benchmark has been frozen or scored. Provenance/license limits remain those of this repository; no external dataset or new distribution license is introduced. See [v3 verification](docs/PHASE_15_READ_QUERIES.md).

Every future dataset must include a version, generation/collection method, source/license records, annotation guidance, split seed and family IDs, deduplication results, evaluation scope, and known limitations. Dataset files and trained artifacts must not imply accuracy before evaluation. See [MODEL_CARD.md](MODEL_CARD.md) for current model status.

The [v4 development corpus](docs/evaluation/phase15/read_queries_v4/README.md) contains 11,168 fictional rows across 7,746 train, 510 calibration, 1,142 threshold-selection and 1,770 evaluation rows. Positive/wrapped negatives remain in one heuristic family partition; exact normalized duplicates are checked. Topics and people are shared across partitions, so unseen-entity generalization is not established. Generated entity spans are assistant annotations; outcomes are simulations. Exposed user strings remain separate regressions and cannot satisfy independent evaluation. The manifest pins source/data hashes; v2/v3 records and human review fields are unchanged.

On 2026-10-10 the user supplied [25 challenge prompts](docs/evaluation/phase15/read_queries_v3/user-challenge-2026-10-10.csv), filling the previously blank human challenge slots. They were excluded from training/tuning and scored against unchanged v3 as a small intent diagnostic. Category labels are user-supplied; entities/outcomes remain assistant drafts and the original 100 examples remain unapproved. See [results and limitations](docs/PHASE_15_USER_CHALLENGE.md). The sample is now exposed and cannot become fresh blind evidence after subsequent tuning.

The [agent training plan](docs/AGENT_TRAINING_PLAN.md) separates deterministic specialists from future learned classifiers/rankers and describes reproducible training, Android inference parity, and opt-in local personalization. No dataset or model has been created by the Phase 4 preference slice.
